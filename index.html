How the pool works

For every game, pick the winner and by how many points they win.

Correct winner, exact margin11
Correct winner, margin off by 1–39
Correct winner, margin off by 4–78
Correct winner, margin off by more than 76
Wrong winner (or a tie)0

Example: Jets 27, Buccaneers 40. "Tampa Bay by 13" earns 11; by 10–16 earns 9; by 6–9 or 17–20 earns 8; by 5 or less or more than 20 earns 6; any Jets pick earns 0.

Deadlines (Mexico City time). Turn in your card before the first kickoff of the week (usually Thursday night). Each game locks at its kickoff. You can change Sunday and Monday picks until Saturday at 11:59 PM, after the Friday injury reports. Everyone's picks for a game become visible once it locks. If you miss the first kickoff you sit out that week.
